Where this problem shows up

Machines break without warning everywhere. Alarms get ignored too.

Spending on AI keeps rising, yet 56% of CEOs saw no significant financial benefit from it last year (PwC, 2026), and a typical AI project takes 2–4 years to pay back (Deloitte, 2025). Unplanned breakdowns are one of the places companies are betting it will.

How it ran

Seven phases, one answer each.

    What I built

    Real data, a working model, documents a team could use.

    The model

    Gradient-boosted trees trained on 10,000 public machine records (UCI AI4I 2020), priced through a cost matrix of catches, misses and false alarms.

    The data path

    Monthly results land in Amazon S3 and flow through Databricks tables, so savings are tracked after launch.

    Two GenAI tests

    Drafting repair orders and reviewing a business case on Amazon Bedrock, each graded against known answers.

    The documents

    A PRD, requirements with 13 acceptance tests, and a two-page decision brief for finance. Read the PRD ↗
