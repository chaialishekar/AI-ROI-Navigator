What I did

Priced every AI in the library against this plant's losses.

The provider's library has nine AI tools. Three fit this plant. For each one I took the improvement it achieved elsewhere, from a published source, and applied it to the plant's own loss. Then I subtracted what the tool costs to set up and run, over three years.

  • Expected view: the middle of each published range.
  • Cautious view: the low end. An option that only wins on the expected view is a risk, not a pick.
  • Every source is labelled by how strong its evidence is, from independent study to vendor claim.
