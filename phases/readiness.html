What I did

Checked whether people will actually use it.

A model that works on a laptop can still fail on the shop floor. Deloitte's 2025 survey found that slow AI payback comes down largely to how people adopt the tools and how their work changes. So before any money was approved, two raters scored the plant on five areas, from 1 (not ready) to 5 (ready).

Average of two raters. Below 2.5 in any area means "fix first". Example ratings.
