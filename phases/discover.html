What I did

Put a price on the problem before talking about AI.

The plant asked for "an AI solution". Before comparing any tools, I wrote down what was actually going wrong and what each problem costs in a year, because an AI can only be worth what the problem it fixes is worth.

  • Listed the three losses the plant reports, and estimated each one in dollars a year.
  • Mapped how a breakdown is handled today (the as-is process), from the machine stopping to the repair.
  • Named who is affected and what each of them needs: operations, finance, maintenance planners, technicians, and data / IT.
